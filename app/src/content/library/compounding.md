# How compounding works

**Simple interest** pays you only on the money you put in.
**Compound interest** also pays you on the interest you have already earned.

## A quick example

Put ₹10,000 away at 8% a year:

- After 1 year: ₹10,800
- After 10 years: about ₹21,600
- After 30 years: about ₹1,00,600

The money did nothing different in year 30 than in year 1. It just had more time.

## The three ingredients

- **Amount**: how much you put in.
- **Rate**: how fast it grows.
- **Time**: how long you leave it. This is usually the one that matters most.

## A warning

Real returns go up and down, and higher returns always come with higher risk. Anyone promising high **guaranteed** returns is a red flag.
