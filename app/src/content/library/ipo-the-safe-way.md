# IPOs, the safe way

An **IPO** (Initial Public Offering) is when a company sells its shares to the public for the first time.

## Before you apply

- Read the offer document filed with SEBI (the RHP). It explains the business, its finances and the risks.
- Use only money you can afford to lose. Listing gains are **never guaranteed**.

## How applying works

1. Apply through your own broker's app or your bank's net banking using **ASBA** (Application Supported by Blocked Amount).
2. Approve the UPI mandate in **your own** UPI app, or let your bank block the amount.
3. If the issue is oversubscribed, allotment is by **computerised lottery**.
4. Allotted? The amount is debited and shares reach your demat account. Not allotted? The block is released.

## Scam signs

- "Guaranteed allotment", "HNI quota", "insider allotment"
- Any "processing fee" or "booking fee"
- "Pre-IPO shares" sold by strangers on WhatsApp or Telegram
- Allotment-status links sent by unknown numbers. Check on the registrar's or exchange's official site instead.
