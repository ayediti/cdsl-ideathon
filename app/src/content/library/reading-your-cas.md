# Reading your CAS

The **Consolidated Account Statement (CAS)** is sent by the depositories. It shows the holdings and transactions in every demat account linked to your PAN, along with your mutual fund holdings, in one place.

## When it comes

You get one for each month with transactions, and at least once every six months otherwise. It usually arrives by email as a password-protected PDF.

## What to check

- **Holdings:** do they match your broker's app?
- **Transactions:** did you make every one of them? Watch for "off-market transfers" you didn't authorise.
- **Personal details:** are your mobile number and email correct?

## If something is wrong

Contact your DP immediately through its **official** app or website, ask for the account to be frozen if needed, and file a written complaint. If it isn't resolved, escalate on **SEBI SCORES** (scores.sebi.gov.in).
