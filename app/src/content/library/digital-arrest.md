# "Digital arrest" scams

A caller, often on a WhatsApp or Skype video call, claims to be from the police, CBI, customs, the narcotics bureau or a court. They say a parcel in your name held drugs, or your Aadhaar was used for money laundering. You are told you are under **"digital arrest"**: you must stay on camera, tell nobody, and transfer money to "prove your innocence".

## The truth

- **There is no such thing as a "digital arrest"** in Indian law.
- Police and investigating agencies don't interrogate people over video calls or ask for money to "verify" accounts.
- Uniforms, offices, ID cards and warrants on a video call are easy to fake.

## What to do

1. **Disconnect.** You don't need anyone's permission to end a call.
2. Talk to a family member or friend straight away. Isolating you is how the scam works.
3. Call **1930** or report at **cybercrime.gov.in**. Report the number on **Sanchar Saathi** (Chakshu).
4. If you've transferred money, call your bank immediately.
