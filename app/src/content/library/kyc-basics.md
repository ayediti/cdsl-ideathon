# KYC basics

**KYC (Know Your Customer)** is how a bank or other financial institution confirms that you are who you say you are before it opens an account for you.

## What is usually needed

- **PAN**: your permanent account number for tax.
- **Proof of identity and address**: commonly Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or driving licence.
- A photograph and signature (or a video / e-KYC process).

## Why it matters

- It stops accounts being opened in fake names.
- Fake ("mule") accounts are how scammers receive and move stolen money.
- It links your financial accounts to you, so you can claim and recover them.

## Watch out

KYC is a common excuse in scams: "Your KYC has expired, update now or your account will be blocked." Banks do not send KYC links by SMS or WhatsApp. Check inside the official app or at the branch.
