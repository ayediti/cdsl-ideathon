# How to check SEBI registration

Anyone can make an ad, a WhatsApp group or a slick app. Only **SEBI-registered** intermediaries are allowed to act as stockbrokers, depository participants, investment advisers or research analysts.

## How to check

1. Go to **sebi.gov.in** yourself (don't use a link someone sent you).
2. Open the list of **recognised intermediaries** and choose the category: stockbroker, investment adviser, research analyst, and so on.
3. Search by name. Check that the **registration number, name and contact details** match what you were given.

## Watch out for

- **Borrowed numbers:** scammers often quote a real registration number that belongs to someone else.
- **"SEBI approved":** SEBI registers firms; it doesn't approve apps or guarantee returns.
- **Paying the right account:** before you pay a broker or adviser, check on sebi.gov.in that the UPI ID or bank account really belongs to the registered firm.
