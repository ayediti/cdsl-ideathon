# Common investor scams

## Tip groups and pump-and-dump

You're added to a WhatsApp or Telegram group run by a "professor" or "SEBI-registered expert". Members post profit screenshots. Then comes an urgent call to buy a small, little-known share. The operators already own it, and they sell to you at the top. Many of the "members" are fake accounts run by the same people.

## Fake trading apps

An app or "institutional account" shows huge profits. When you try to withdraw, you're told to pay "tax", "fees" or "margin" first. The profits were never real.

## Fake advisers and finfluencers

Paid tips from someone who is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser or research analyst. Check registration before paying anyone.

## Fake regulators and police

Calls, letters or "notices" claiming to be from SEBI, a depository, the police or the courts, demanding fines or "verification payments". Regulators don't collect money this way.

## The common threads

- Guaranteed or very high returns
- Urgency and secrecy
- Borrowed authority: logos, uniforms, celebrity videos
- Requests for OTPs, TPINs, app installs or payments to personal UPI IDs
