# Why banks exist

Keeping cash at home feels simple, but it has hidden costs.

## Cash at home

- Earns nothing, while prices keep rising.
- Can be lost, stolen or damaged.
- Leaves no record of where it went.
- Is easy to spend on impulse.

## A bank account

- Is regulated, and deposits are insured up to a limit (₹5 lakh per depositor per bank under DICGC).
- Pays interest on savings.
- Gives you a statement: a record of every rupee in and out.
- Lets you pay safely by UPI, card or transfer.

A bank is not just a safe. It is a system that gives you records, protection and options.
