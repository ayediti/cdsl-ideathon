# Pension and KYC update scams

Every November, many pensioners submit a **life certificate** to keep their pension flowing. Scammers use this, and "KYC update" deadlines, to make urgent calls:

"Your life certificate is not updated. Your pension will stop. I can update it on the phone right now."

## How it really works

- Life certificates are submitted at your bank or pension office, through doorstep banking, or digitally through **Jeevan Pramaan** using your own fingerprint or face authentication.
- KYC updates happen at your branch or in your bank's official app.
- Nobody genuine will ask you to read out an **OTP**, PIN or password.

## Read the OTP message

An OTP message usually says what it is for. If it says "adding beneficiary" or shows an amount, someone is trying to move your money.

## If you get a call

Hang up. Call your bank or pension office on the number in your passbook or pension papers. If you have shared an OTP, call your bank and **1930** immediately.
