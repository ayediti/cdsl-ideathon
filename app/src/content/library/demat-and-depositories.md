# Demat accounts and depositories

## Three accounts, three jobs

1. **Bank account:** your money.
2. **Trading account:** with a stockbroker, to place buy and sell orders.
3. **Demat account:** where the shares you own are held electronically, in your name.

## Depositories

India has two depositories: **CDSL** (Central Depository Services (India) Ltd) and **NSDL** (National Securities Depository Ltd). They keep the official record of who owns which securities.

## Depository Participants (DPs)

You don't open an account with a depository directly. You go through a **Depository Participant**: a SEBI-registered broker, bank or financial firm acting as the depository's agent.

## Keep it safe

- Keep your mobile number and email updated with your DP, so you get every alert.
- Add a **nominee**, or formally opt out.
- Never share your **TPIN**, OTPs or login with anyone, including people claiming to be from your broker.
- Read your **Consolidated Account Statement (CAS)** whenever it arrives.
