# Deepfakes and voice clones

AI can now copy a face or a voice from a few seconds of video posted online. Scammers use this for:

- **Celebrity investment ads:** a famous businessperson or news anchor "recommends" a trading app or scheme.
- **Family emergencies:** a voice note or call that sounds exactly like your grandchild, asking for urgent money.
- **Fake officials:** video calls from "police" or "officers".

## Don't judge the video, judge the request

Glitches like odd lip-sync are getting harder to spot. Ask instead:

- Is someone asking for **money, OTPs or app installs**?
- Is there **urgency or secrecy**?
- Is the promise **too good to be true**?

## How to verify

- **Call back** on a number you already have, not the one that contacted you.
- Agree a **family safe word** for emergencies.
- Check claims about government schemes on official sites or **PIB Fact Check**.
