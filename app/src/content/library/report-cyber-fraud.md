# Reporting cyber fraud

If you lose money to a scam, speed matters. Report within the first hour if you can.

## Steps

1. **Call 1930**, the national cyber-crime helpline, or file a complaint at **cybercrime.gov.in**.
2. **Call your bank** on its official number and ask them to block your card, UPI and net banking.
3. **Change passwords and PINs**, and remove devices you do not recognise.
4. **Report the fake number, SMS or call** on the Sanchar Saathi portal (Chakshu).
5. **Save evidence**: screenshots, transaction reference numbers, the scammer's number and UPI ID.

## Remember

Being targeted is not your fault. Scams are run by organised groups who practise their scripts every day. Reporting helps stop the money and protects the next person.
