# Reporting investor fraud

Speed matters. Report as soon as you notice something wrong.

## Money lost to a scam

1. Call **1930**, the national cyber-fraud helpline, or report at **cybercrime.gov.in**.
2. Call your bank on its official number to block cards, UPI and net banking if needed.
3. Keep evidence: screenshots, transaction IDs, phone numbers, UPI IDs.

## Shares or demat account at risk

1. Contact your **broker or DP** through its official app, website or number.
2. Ask for your **trading or demat account to be frozen**.
3. File a written complaint with them.
4. Not resolved? Escalate on **SEBI SCORES** (scores.sebi.gov.in).

## Fake calls, messages and groups

- Report fraud calls and messages on **Sanchar Saathi** (Chakshu).
- Report and exit the group or account on the platform itself.
