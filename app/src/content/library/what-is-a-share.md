# What is a share?

A **share** is a small piece of ownership in a company. If a company is split into 1,00,000 shares and you own 100, you own 0.1% of it.

## Why companies sell shares

Companies need money to grow. Instead of borrowing, they can sell part of the business to the public. The first time they do this is an **IPO** (Initial Public Offering). After that, shares are bought and sold between investors on a **stock exchange**, mainly the NSE and the BSE in India.

## Why prices move

- Prices come from trading: what buyers will pay and what sellers will accept.
- They react to company profits, news, the economy, interest rates and plain emotion.
- **Nobody can guarantee a share price.** Anyone who promises "sure-shot" gains is either guessing or lying.

## Who keeps it fair

**SEBI** (the Securities and Exchange Board of India) regulates the market. It makes the rules and registers stockbrokers, depository participants, investment advisers and research analysts. It does not set prices or approve apps.
